About SUNY New Paltz

A campus where curiosity and community come together.

Learn. Create. Connect.

SUNY New Paltz is a public university located in the Hudson Valley of New York. The campus combines a strong liberal arts foundation with professional programs, creative disciplines, research opportunities, and an active campus community.


With a location close to the mountains, historic villages, and the greater New York metropolitan area, students have opportunities to learn both inside and outside the classroom.
